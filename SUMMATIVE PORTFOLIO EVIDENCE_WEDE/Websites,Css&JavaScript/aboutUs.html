About Us

Home of Hope began as a safe haven for children affected by abandonment, abuse, neglect, and HIV/AIDS. Today, we continue our mission to provide a nurturing and secure environment focused on education, health, and sustainable growth.

Our Mission

To create a safe and supportive space where every child can thrive, access education, and build a hopeful future filled with opportunity.

How You Can Help

  • Donate monthly or make a once-off contribution
  • Sponsor a child’s education, meals, or wellbeing
  • Volunteer your time, skills, or professional services
