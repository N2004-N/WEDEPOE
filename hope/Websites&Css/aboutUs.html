About Us

Home of Hope is a Non-Profit Organization (NPO) based in Johannesburg, dedicated to providing shelter, education, and care for vulnerable children.

The organisation started as an interim ‘place of safety’ for children who were abandoned, violently abused, raped, hungry, neglected due to poverty, infected with HIV and AIDS, or born bearing the effects of excessive drug and alcohol abuse during pregnancy.

Our mission is to create a safe environment where every child can thrive, learn, and build a hopeful future.
